Sources and credits

The numbers match the note calls in the articles. Web pages were consulted on 9 and 12 October 2026.

This work starts from the cryptogram listed by S. Tomokiyo on his site Cryptiana [2, 3], devoted to the history of cryptography, whose pages on Napoleon's great ciphers guided the whole search (users of the Great Cipher, codes of 1812–1813, references to Vilcoq).

It also relies on the list of unsolved ciphers and the notes published by Daniel Bourdeau [4] (CC BY 4.0), on the photographs of the Great Cipher table published by Jean-François Bouchaudy [5], and on the transcriptions of Imperial codes by Tant, published by the ARCSI [6].

Thanks to all.
